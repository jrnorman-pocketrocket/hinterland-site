7. Using the trailer

You must:

  • keep the load under 400 kg and properly restrained, with nothing sticking out past the cage;

  • cover loose loads (green waste, rubbish) with a tarp or net;

  • close and latch the rear tailgate before moving, every time. Never drive with the tailgate down;

  • attach the safety chain and check the lights before each trip;

  • follow all Queensland road rules and towing laws.

You must not:

  • carry people or animals in the trailer;

  • carry hazardous, flammable or illegal goods, asbestos, or anything that leaks or stains (e.g. wet concrete, oil, chemicals);

  • take the trailer off-road, onto beaches, or outside South East Queensland without our written OK;

  • tow while affected by alcohol or drugs;

  • sub-hire, lend or modify the trailer.
