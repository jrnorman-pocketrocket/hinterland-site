We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the date above and provide notice as required by applicable law. We encourage you to review this policy regularly.

Personal information we collect or process

When we use the term "personal information," we mean information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to you or another person. Personal information does not include information that is collected anonymously or that has been de-identified so that it cannot identify or be reasonably linked to you. Depending on how you interact with the Services, where you live, and as permitted or required by applicable law, we may collect or process:

  • Contact details including your name, address, billing address, phone number, and email address;

  • Financial and payment information including payment card details handled by our payment processor, payment confirmation, transaction references, and saved-method metadata such as card brand and last four digits;

  • Account information including preferences, settings, and sign-in related security signals;

  • Booking and transaction information including products hired, location, dates, pricing, deposits, returns, exchanges, cancellations, and past bookings;

  • Identity or insurance verification details where required for a hire (documents are typically processed by specialist verification providers; we keep status and related booking metadata);

  • Communications including information you include when contacting support or otherwise communicating with us;

  • Photos, answers, or other content you submit in quizzes, checklists, or pickup/return forms;

  • Device and usage information including IP address, browser or device type, pages viewed, links clicked, referrer, approximate location derived from network information, and how you navigate the Services; and

  • Location-related signals where enabled for a hire (for example map search inputs, or GPS-related timestamps associated with pickup, return, or tracked equipment).

Some of the categories above may be considered sensitive personal information under applicable law, such as a government-issued identification number or image used for identity or insurance verification. We use sensitive personal information only to verify identity, prevent fraud, comply with law, and provide the hire you request, and not to infer characteristics about you. Where applicable law gives you a right to limit our use of sensitive personal information, you can exercise that right using the contact details below.
